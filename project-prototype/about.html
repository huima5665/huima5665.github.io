About the Project

This projects compiles my design of everyday eating routines. The routines are dissected into two main parts: preparation cycles and cooking procedures, and they are manifestations of the goals I have: efficiency, taste, nutrition, aesthetics. The aim of the project is to operationalize regularity: how to maintain the rhythm of meals and what it means to make it both practical and pleasurable.

About the Project and Me

For years, during the academic semesters, I’ve had the habit of two meals a day: one around noon and one in the evening. This schedule mirrors aspects of my life: my late mornings shaped by insomnia, the flexibility of being a student who manages to avoid morning classes most of the time, and a balance between structure and spontaneity.

My meals also reflect my concerns: how to fit meal prep and cooking in a pleasantly and efficiently way into my schedule; maximize the pleasure of the taste with my limited cooking skills; treating the plate as both a nutritional system, keep track of my intake of fiber, protein, vitamins, and carbohydrates;  also treating the meals like a small aesthetic composition.
